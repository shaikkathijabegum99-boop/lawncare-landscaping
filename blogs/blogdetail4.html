Seasonal Lawn Guide

Keep your garden healthy year-round

GreenScape Experts • 7 min read

Introduction

Lawn care changes with seasons. Understanding seasonal needs helps maintain a healthy, green, and strong garden throughout the year.

Spring Care

  • Remove winter debris
  • Apply fertilizer
  • Start regular watering

Summer Care

  • Increase watering frequency
  • Mow grass regularly
  • Prevent weed growth

Monsoon Care

  • Improve drainage
  • Avoid overwatering
  • Control fungal growth

Winter Care

  • Reduce watering
  • Protect grass from frost
  • Light pruning
“A healthy lawn is maintained, not created overnight.”
