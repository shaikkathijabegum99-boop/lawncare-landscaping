Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our Landscape Architecture services.

How long does a landscape design project take?

Timelines vary based on project size and complexity, but most residential projects take between 2 and 8 weeks for design development.

Do you provide 3D landscape designs?

Yes. We create detailed 3D visualizations and renderings to help clients clearly understand the final design.

Can you redesign existing landscapes?

Absolutely. We specialize in upgrading outdated landscapes and transforming underutilized outdoor spaces.

Do you handle project implementation?

Yes. Our team can manage the complete installation process and coordinate contractors when required.

Are sustainable designs available?

Sustainability is a core part of our approach. We incorporate native plants, efficient irrigation systems, and eco-friendly materials whenever possible.

Do you work on commercial projects?

Yes. We provide landscape architecture services for offices, hotels, retail centers, campuses, and public spaces.
